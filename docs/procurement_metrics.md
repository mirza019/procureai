# Procurement metrics

- Supplier share = supplier spend / total spend.
- HHI = sum of squared supplier shares × 10,000.
- Purchase price variance = quantity × (actual unit price − expected unit price).
- OTD = deliveries with actual date on/before planned date / deliveries received.
- Supplier score = 25% quality + 25% delivery + 20% cost + 20% inverse risk + 10% commercial.

Inputs are constrained to 0–100 and score weights must sum to 1. API responses disclose the
calculation method and source period; AI providers never recalculate these values.
